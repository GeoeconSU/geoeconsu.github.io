Who We Are

Narrative intelligence is abundant.
Evidence is scarce.

Most geopolitical advice is opinion dressed as analysis. The Geoeconomic Strategy Unit is built on a different premise: sovereign risk can be measured, tested, and forecast — and the output should change decisions, not just inform them.

The Geoeconomic Strategy Unit is a practitioner-led, research-driven firm that translates the complexity of geopolitics and global economics into clear, actionable intelligence for business leaders. We combine on-the-ground insight, policy analysis, and quantitative frameworks to help executives decide where to deploy capital, how to structure business models, and when to exercise strategic patience.

Our mission is to improve capital mobility and unlock cross-border growth by highlighting risks, aligning incentives, and sequencing action across high-growth trade corridors. We draw on long-standing relationships with ministries, investors, media, and corporations to translate analysis into financing structures and risk-mitigation strategies.

#01 Empirical

Every claim traces to data. Our frameworks are built on fifteen years of observations across 156 countries — with the causal tests to back them, not anecdotes from the ground.

#02 Quantitative

RAO scores, regime classification, and trajectory signals — outputs you can put in a model, not just a memo. Intelligence that earns its place in a spreadsheet.

#03 Actionable

Analysis is only intelligence when it changes a decision. Every deliverable ends where it should begin: what to do, where to do it, and when to move.

Core IP

The RAO Framework

Risk-Adjusted Opportunity — a quantitative scoring system measuring governance quality, growth trajectory, and fragility risk across the sovereign universe. Our proprietary engine processes continuous data signals to deliver scores on demand.

15yrs Panel Data 2010 – 2025

60/40 Weighting Opportunity / Fragility

The 60/40 Weighting

Our algorithm balances two competing forces to produce a composite RAO score (0–10):

60% Opportunity

GDP growth, trade balances, policy incentives, and market entry feasibility.

40% Fragility

Sovereign spreads, currency volatility, conflict risks, and regulatory shifts.

Beyond country-level data, our system allows for on-demand, sector-specific scoring. Whether you operate in Fintech, Renewable Energy, or Critical Minerals, we isolate the regulatory environments and supply chain risks that matter to your industry.
