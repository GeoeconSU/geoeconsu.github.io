The Geoeconomic Risk Barometer is the flagship research of the Geoeconomic Strategy Unit. It gives leaders a decision-grade view of where cross-border investment can be executed in the near and medium term, blending hard indicators with regional insight and corridor-level analysis.

The Q3 2026 edition covers nine regions, from North America to Latin America, and closes with key findings and a Q4 outlook.

The reopening of key maritime corridors in the Middle East will be a key determinant of growth in Q4. Grid connectivity emerges as another constraint, especially for energy-intensive investments in AI.
