IN FOCUS   /   MANUFACTURING & SHIPBUILDING

From policy ambition
to shipyard delivery.

India’s domestic shipbuilding output rose 41% in 2025. A US$7.27bn support package, emerging international partnerships and new commercial orders are building momentum. The next test is turning that support into competitive costs, local sourcing and on-time delivery.
