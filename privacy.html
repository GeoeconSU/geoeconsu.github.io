Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 July 2026

Draft for review. This describes how the platform currently handles personal data. It should be reviewed against your GDPR/UK-GDPR obligations — including your legal entity, data controller details and any registered representative — before general availability.

What we collect

  • Account data — your name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile Google returns.
  • Plan data — the plan you hold or have requested, and the list of markets attached to your account.
  • Usage data — which tabs and countries you open, sign-in events and upgrade prompts shown. This is collected through Google Analytics for Firebase.

We do not collect payment card details through the platform.

Why we use it

  • To authenticate you and enforce what your plan gives you access to.
  • To respond to Personal and Enterprise access requests.
  • To understand how the product is used so we can improve it.
  • To contact you about your account and material changes to the service.

Where it is stored

Authentication and account records are held in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Analytics events are processed by Google Analytics for Firebase. These are processed on Google infrastructure and may involve transfers outside your country under Google's standard contractual clauses.

Who can see it

Access to member records is limited to GSU staff with an administrative role, through an authenticated console. Database rules restrict what each role can read and write. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers named above.

How long we keep it

Account records are kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for legitimate business and legal purposes. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. To exercise any of these, contact us at the address below.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in, to remember your saved markets, and to carry a plan choice made at registration through to account creation. Analytics uses cookies to distinguish sessions.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: info@geoeconsu.com.
