Partnership Blueprint

IGPL Africa Swing
Sponsorship Strategy

A comprehensive framework for securing cash and in-kind partners by aligning tournament legs with sponsor business objectives across the India-Africa corridor.

Countries: South Africa • Mauritius • Republic of the Congo | Prepared By: GSU | Geoeconomic Strategy Unit

Executive Summary

Objective

Secure a mix of cash + in-kind partners by aligning each tournament leg with the sponsor categories most likely to pay for it, and running a focused, country-wise outreach sprint.

Top Priority

Companies with big budgets that can write a check for IGPL.

Strategic Approach

  • Sell each leg with a different "reason to buy"
  • Anchor the tour with 2-3 Gold partners
  • Use one consistent tiering framework (Gold/Silver/Bronze)
  • Run outreach in two waves (Anchor → Co-sponsors)
  • Make the ask concrete (hospitality inventory + brand assets)

Sponsorship Architecture

Three-tier framework with clear benefits at each level

Gold

Anchor Partners

Largest cheque / highest certainty impact

What They Get:
  • Naming rights ("IGPL presented by...")
  • Hosted Pro-Am entries, VIP tables, private dinners
  • 3-5 premium content deliverables
  • Category exclusivity

Silver

Category Co-Sponsors

Strong fit, controlled spend

What They Get:
  • Category naming + branded lounge
  • Hospitality block (VIP seats + sponsor client day)
  • Co-branded content + lead-gen hooks

Bronze

Activation Partners

Volume pipeline; fast closes

What They Get:
  • On-ground branding + 1 activation
  • Social content bundle + photo rights
  • Prize sponsorship opportunities
Important Note on Alcohol Partnerships

Keep alcohol partnerships experience-led (VIP dinners/tastings) and run them only within local compliance and IGPL brand guidelines.
