About the Geoeconomic Strategy Unit

A practitioner-led, research-driven firm that turns geopolitics and global economics into clear intelligence for business leaders.

Who we are. We combine on-the-ground insight, policy analysis, and strategic experience to help you decide where to deploy capital, how to structure business models, and when to exercise strategic patience.

Our mission. To improve capital mobility and unlock cross-border growth by highlighting risks, aligning incentives, and sequencing action across high-growth trade corridors.

What we publish. The quarterly Geoeconomic Risk Barometer, country briefs, corridor views with triggers to watch, and interim alerts when conditions materially shift.

What we can do for you

  1. Structured intelligence to keep your leadership informed as conditions shift and opportunities open. Published quarterly, with interim updates when conditions materially shift.

  2. Focused country assessments grounded in the Risk-Adjusted Opportunity score, with the triggers to watch.

  3. Trade-corridor views with triggers to watch, and interim alerts on material changes.

  4. Direct engagement with your leadership team to translate intelligence into concrete decisions and governance steps.

  5. Sequenced plans for entering or expanding in a market, calibrated to your time horizon and risk appetite.

  6. Planning for best, base and worst cases tied to concrete triggers such as elections and policy moves.

  7. Bespoke deep-dives for specific deals, corridors or investment decisions, including early-warning checks.

  8. Sequencing decisions and investments, and building structural resilience into your risk framework.
